# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share personal data when you use our learning platform (the "Platform"), in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who we are

We are the operator of this Platform and the data controller responsible for your personal data. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us through the Platform.

## What personal data we collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect the following personal data: first name and last name, school or educational institution, email address, date of birth, login and account information such as encrypted passwords, and user role (student or teacher). If you are under 16 years old, we may also collect the email address of your parent or legal guardian for consent verification. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

## How we collect your data

We collect data when you create an account, use the Platform, communicate with us, or when your parent or legal guardian provides consent on your behalf.

## Why we use your data (legal basis)

We process personal data only when we have a valid legal basis under the GDPR, including performance of a contract to create and manage your account and provide access to the Platform, legal obligation to comply with applicable laws, legitimate interests to ensure platform security and improve the service, and consent when required, especially for minors or optional features.

## Children and parental consent

Protecting children's privacy is very important to us. Users under the age of 16 may only use the Platform with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. We require verifiable parental consent before activating such accounts. Parents or legal guardians may contact us at any time to review, modify, or request deletion of the child's data.

## How we use your data

We use personal data only to operate and manage the Platform, provide educational services, communicate with users and guardians, maintain security and prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use personal data for advertising purposes.

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data only with authorized administrators or teachers when necessary for educational purposes, with service providers that help operate the Platform under strict data protection agreements, or when required by law.

## Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to maintain your account, provide educational services, and meet legal or regulatory requirements. When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

## Data security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate or incomplete data, request deletion of your data, restrict or object to certain processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent at any time when processing is based on consent. Parents or legal guardians may exercise these rights on behalf of minors.

## International users

Our Platform is operated from the European Union. If you access the Platform from outside the EU, your data will still be protected under the GDPR.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the updated date will be shown at the top.

## Contact and complaints

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us through the Platform. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Portugal, this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

## Final note

By using the Platform, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you are under 16 years old, you confirm that you have permission from your parent or legal guardian.
